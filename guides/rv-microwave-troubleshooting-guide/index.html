Field notes

Turntable Spins, No Heat

This isolates the fault to the magnetron or high-voltage circuit, not the control board or motor.

Unit Won’t Power On at All

Check the door switch/interlock first — a misaligned or failed door switch will kill power to the whole unit as a safety measure, and is a far more common culprit than a dead control board.

Convection Mode Doesn’t Work

If microwave mode works fine but convection (the oven-style heating element and fan) doesn’t, the fault is isolated to that circuit specifically — usually the convection heating element or its relay, not shared compon.

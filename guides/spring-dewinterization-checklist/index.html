Spring de-winterization checklist

De-winterizing is winterizing in reverse, plus a damage walk. A coach that sat through freeze-thaw needs more than pink flushed out of the faucets.

Do not drink from the system until bypass is back to normal, color and smell are gone, and you have sanitized. A “quick flush” that leaves the heater in bypass or pink in the traps is how people get a surprise on the first weekend.

Plumbing, in order

Close what you opened

Low-point drains closed. Water-heater drain plug or anode back in — snug, not wrecked threads.

Bypass off

Put the water heater back in normal flow before you fill. Leaving it in bypass means the tank stays empty or air-locked and the first hot shower is a mess.

Flush until clear

Every faucet hot and cold, shower, toilet, outside shower. No pink tint, no antifreeze smell.

Sanitize

Fresh tank and lines: about 1/4 cup household bleach per 15 gallons of tank capacity, fill, sit several hours, then flush until chlorine is gone. Do not sanitize into a full water-heater tank full of leftover antifreeze — finish the flush first.

Then walk the freeze-thaw damage

Pressure test

Once the system holds water, look under sinks, at low points, and around the pump. Hairline PEX splits show up as drips, not drama.

Roof and seals

Freeze-thaw opens last fall’s “small” gaps. Check roof, windows, and compartment doors before the first rain trip.

Batteries

Lithium stored mid-SoC, lead-acid should have been on a maintainer. Load-check before you trust a night off-grid.

Tires, LP, pests

Set pressures. Cycle furnace and water heater on LP. Look for chewed PEX and wiring — mice work winter hours.

Pair with the winterization guide if you are unsure which method was used last fall. If the coach was blow-out only, traps may still be dry — refill them. If it was antifreeze, expect more flush time.

Owner checklist
  • Confirm bypass valve position with a photo from last fall if you have one
  • Flush every fixture until water is clear
  • Sanitize after color and smell are gone
  • Pressure-check for winter splits before you leave the driveway
  • Walk roof seals and cycle LP appliances once

FAQ

Can I skip sanitize?

You can. You should not drink that water. Flush plus sanitize is the standard after antifreeze.

Pink still after 20 minutes?

Keep going. Bypass mistakes and water-heater pockets hide pink. Do not drink yet.

Found a split line?

Stop the pump, isolate, repair, then finish de-winterize. Booking plumbing first is cheaper than a soaked subfloor.

Trip-prep vs this list?

This list is water and a damage walk. Trip-prep is the broader go-to-travel check. Ask which quote you need.

Seasonal guide

Spring de-winterization checklist

De-winterizing is winterizing in reverse, plus a full damage check — a rig that sat through winter needs more than just the antifreeze flushed before that first trip.

Source: WP Field Guide. The live article is filed under seasonal / travel (including Michigan-specific notes). Corridor service: Montana · Wyoming · Idaho · Washington — case-by-case beyond.

Field notes

Flushing the plumbing system

Run RV-safe antifreeze completely out of every fixture — each faucet (hot and cold), the shower, the toilet, and the outside shower if equipped — until water runs clear with no pink tint.

  • Sanitize the fresh water tank and lines afterward. Sitting antifreeze residue plus a tank that’s been dry for months is a combination worth a proper flush and sanitize, not a rinse. WP procedure: tank sanitizing guide (~¼ cup unscented household bleach per 15 gallons, run until you smell chlorine at every faucet, sit 4–12 hours / overnight, then drain and rinse).
  • Check the water heater bypass valve is switched back to normal operation before filling the tank — an easy step to forget after months away from the system.

Full reverse procedure, including bleach ratio and leak check, is also on the docs winterization guide.

Checking for winter damage

Roof & seals

Inspect for cracking caused by freeze-thaw cycling — water that got into a small seal gap in fall can expand when it freezes and widen the gap significantly by spring.

Batteries

Check condition and state of charge. A battery that wasn’t maintained through storage can be permanently damaged by a deep winter discharge.

Propane appliances

Test them. Run the furnace and water heater through a cycle before you need heat on the road.

Tires

Check pressure and condition. The live guide points at the WP tire aging guide if the rig sat on the same spot all winter.

From the live Field Guide. Pink still in a fixture means keep flushing. Do not drink until color and smell are gone and the tank has been sanitized.

DIY vs book a visit

Reasonable DIY

Flush, bypass reset, sanitize, and a visual roof/tire/battery walk-around if you winterized the coach yourself and know the valve layout.

Book when

You want de-winterize plus a damage check done on the corridor. Published winterization / trip-prep flats live on pricing (travel trailer vs 5th-wheel / motorhome). Labor context $150/hr.

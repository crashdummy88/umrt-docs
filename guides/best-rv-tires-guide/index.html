Field notes

Tire choice on an RV or trailer isn’t just about brand — it’s about matching the tire’s category, load range, and speed rating to how the vehicle is actually built and used. ST (Special Trailer) tires are engineered specifically for trailer axles — stiffer sidewalls to resist trailer sway, built for the different stress pattern of a towed axle versus a drive axle. LT (Light Truck) tires are built for powered, steered axles on motorhomes and tow vehicles, where the load and stress patterns differ from a trailer’s.

Load range and matching the axle rating

Load range (a letter rating like C, D, or E) indicates the tire’s maximum load capacity at a given pressure — it needs to meet or exceed your axle’s GAWR, not just “seem about right” compared to the old tire.

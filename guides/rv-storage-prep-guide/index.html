Field notes

A rig parked for the off-season isn’t just idle — it’s a target for rodents looking for shelter, batteries that drain and degrade unattended, and seals that fail quietly with nobody around to notice.

Keeping rodents out

Mice can enter through gaps smaller than a dime, so check and seal any opening around plumbing and wiring penetrations, the furnace and water heater exterior vents, and slide-out gaps before storage.

Battery tending and seal checks

A battery left connected and unattended can deep-discharge over a long storage period, which shortens its life or ruins it outright — a battery tender/maintainer keeps it at proper float charge without overcharging.

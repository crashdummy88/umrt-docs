RV OWNER’S FIELD GUIDE · SEASONAL, TRAVEL & MICHIGAN-SPECIFIC The Upper Peninsula is one of the most scenic legs of our route — it’s also one of the most remote, with lake-effect snow patterns and long stretches between services that catch travelers off guard.

Lake-effect snow and shoulder-season timing

Lake-effect snow off Lake Superior can produce intense, localized snowfall that’s hard to predict from a general regional forecast — conditions can change dramatically within a short drive, especially near the shoreli.

Remote stretches and rig prep

Services thin out considerably between the larger U.P. towns — fuel up and don’t let tank levels run low between known stops.

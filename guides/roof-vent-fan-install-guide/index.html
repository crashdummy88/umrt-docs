Field notes

A roof vent fan is one of the highest-value van build upgrades — and one of the easiest installs to turn into a future leak if the sealing isn’t done right. The opening needs to land between roof ribs/framing, not through one, and the flange needs full contact with the roof surface all the way around — a corner that’s not fully bedded in sealant is exactly where water finds its way in months later, often nowhere near where the leak eventually shows up inside.

Sealant Choice Matters

Self-leveling lap sealant is the standard for horizontal roof penetrations — it’s designed to stay flexible through roof expansion/contraction.

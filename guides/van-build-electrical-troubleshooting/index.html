Field notes

DIY conversions have the widest range of electrical problems we see — because every build is different. Here’s how to think through a fault when there’s no factory wiring diagram to fall back on. Factory RVs follow a manufacturer’s wiring diagram and standardized components. Van builds and skoolie conversions are one-off systems, often assembled from parts sourced from several different brands and installed by several different people over time — sometimes including the owner.

Why Custom Builds Fail Differently

Factory RVs follow a manufacturer’s wiring diagram and standardized components.

Common Symptoms & What They Usually Mean

Before You Start Troubleshooting

If there’s no wiring diagram, make one — even a rough sketch of every component, fuse, and connection point saves enormous time on every future issue. Label circuits at the panel.

When to Stop and Call a Professional

Mixed-brand systems with no documentation, repeated intermittent faults that resist basic checks, or any build where you’re not fully confident in the existing wiring are worth a professional once-over — especially be.

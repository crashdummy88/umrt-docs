Field notes

Two different jobs that get confused constantly — resealing seams and penetrations, and coating the whole roof. Here’s the difference. Every roof vent, AC unit, antenna, and seam is a potential leak point, and the sealant around them degrades faster than the roof membrane itself — this is the maintenance that actually prevents leaks. A visual inspection twice a year, checking every penetration for cracked or lifted sealant, catches most problems before they become interior damage.

Resealing Seams & Penetrations

Every roof vent, AC unit, antenna, and seam is a potential leak point, and the sealant around them degrades faster than the roof membrane itself — this is the maintenance that actually prevents leaks.

Full Roof Coatings

A liquid-applied coating over the entire roof surface, marketed as extending roof life and improving UV/heat reflection.

Inspect Before You Coat

A proper pre-coating inspection checks the roof decking underneath for soft spots (a sign of existing water damage) before anything gets sealed over it.

Sizing to your actual loads

Size for your highest simultaneous load plus the startup surge of motor-driven appliances like a compressor fridge or AC unit — surge draw is often several times the running wattage.

From the live Field Guide

Modified sine wave inverters are cheaper but produce a rougher waveform that can cause humming, reduced motor efficiency, and malfunction in sensitive electronics. For most modern RVs, pure sine is the practical standard. Combined inverter/chargers add battery charging from shore power in the same unit — fewer components than a separate inverter and converter.

Work the math in the inverter sizing guide before you buy. Victron’s inverter/charger line integrates with the rest of a Victron DC system — the unit we run on certified installs. Renogy pure sine is a straightforward option when you are not building the full Victron ecosystem.

Electrical & Power · Guide

Best RV inverter/chargers

The brand on the side of the box matters less than waveform, surge, idle draw, and whether the charger side matches the bank you actually have.

An inverter turns battery DC into AC. An inverter/charger also charges that bank from shore or a generator. Combined units cut duplicate boxes and let transfer happen in one place — if they are programmed for the chemistry and the generator you have.

What to decide before a brand

Pure sine

Clean AC close to shore power. Required for CPAP, many tool chargers, and modern appliance motors.

Modified sine

Budget waveform. Hum, heat, and risk to sensitive gear. Not what we put in a living coach.

Size to loads

Do the sizing math first. Surge of the fridge plus microwave is the usual trap.

Charger or not

If you already have a healthy converter and only need occasional AC, a standalone inverter can be enough. If you are rebuilding the power wall, an inverter/charger is the cleaner core.

What we install

Victron MultiPlus / MultiPlus-II

Pure sine, configurable charger, VE.Bus ecosystem, app history. The unit we commission on Victron DC systems. Victron Professional Certified Installer.

Not a trophy shelf

We do not chase every SKU. If the coach is not a Victron stack, a quality pure-sine inverter/charger with a published lithium profile still beats a mystery Amazon brick.

Renogy and similar

Fine as a straightforward pure-sine box when you are not building the full Victron network. Still size cable and fusing like a real install.

Generator + inverter

The charger must accept the generator’s voltage and frequency. Cheap generators plus picky chargers is a known fight — test before you leave the site.

Owner checklist
  • Pure sine unless you have a documented exception
  • Match charger profile to LiFePO4 or AGM
  • Confirm transfer / grounding with the coach AC panel
  • Leave service access to the AC and DC lugs
  • Read fault codes before swapping the unit — many “dead inverters” are low-voltage or overload

FAQ

Inverter vs inverter/charger?

Inverter only makes AC. The combo also charges from shore/generator and usually handles transfer.

Will it run the microwave?

If continuous and surge ratings say so, and the bank can deliver the DC current.

Why is it hot in the bay?

Idle losses and poor airflow. A sealed basement with no intake will thermal-limit a good unit.

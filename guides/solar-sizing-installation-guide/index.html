RV solar sizing & installation

Most failed solar jobs were sized to the roof, not to the loads. Add up watt-hours first. Panels, bank, and controller come after the number is honest.

A compressor fridge (often 600–900 Wh/day), CPAP (30–60 Wh), lighting (100–200 Wh), pump and fans (100–150 Wh), plus inverter idle and any AC loads, is a real daily budget. Boondocking coaches commonly land between 1,500 and 3,000 Wh/day. Write yours down. Do not guess from a Facebook “400 W is plenty.”

Order of operations

1. Daily watt-hours

List every always-on and evening load. Use nameplate watts × hours, then add 20% for inverter and wiring loss if those loads are AC.

2. Battery bank

The bank carries you through night and weather. Size usable capacity to 1.5–2 days with no sun. Lithium usable is most of the nameplate; lead-acid is not.

3. Array

Harvest has to replace the day’s use plus charge inefficiency in the hours of real sun you actually get on the corridor — not a desert brochure.

4. Controller and wire

MPPT current rating and Voc headroom first. Then cable gauge for the run length. Voltage drop is a silent power thief.

What the install actually is

Roof mounts and seals

A bad panel seal is a future leak, not a solar problem. Bond into structure, drip-loop the cable, and leave a way to inspect the seal next season.

Wire routing

Size for current and length. Fuse at the source. Strain-relieve every gland. Label both ends. Do not pinch cable in a slide or compartment door.

Controller and chemistry

The charge profile must match LiFePO4 vs AGM. A lead-acid profile on lithium leaves capacity on the table or fights the BMS.

Commissioning

We confirm Voc, Imp, absorb voltage, and that the app/monitor shows the array you think you bought. Victron installs get that logged.

Owner checklist
  • Write the daily Wh list before shopping
  • Size the bank to 1.5–2 no-sun days of usable capacity
  • Check controller Voc in the cold — panel voltage rises when it is cold
  • Fuse the array and the battery side independently
  • Photograph roof penetrations after sealant skins

FAQ

Can I add panels later?

Yes if you left controller headroom and roof space. Adding a second mismatched string without a second MPPT is how people clip harvest.

Do I need lithium first?

Not required. Lithium changes how much usable bank you get per pound. The Wh math still comes first.

Why is my array “weak”?

Shade, dirty glass, undersized wire, a PWM on a high-Voc string, or a controller in the wrong chemistry profile.

DIY vs book?

Owners can rack a portable kit. Sealed roof work and lithium commissioning are the usual book line.

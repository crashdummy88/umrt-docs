Field notes

Identify your roof skin before buying coatings or cleaners. Wrong chemistry can stain or void care guidance.

Common skins

TPO / EPDM membrane

Flexible rubber/thermoplastic membranes. Inspect seams, lap sealant, and every vent/AC curb. UV and foot traffic matter.

Metal

Can shed water well but needs solid fastener and seal discipline at every screw and joint.

Fiberglass

Often smoother; still depends on sealant at openings and wall joints.

Coatings

Only after diagnosis and compatible prep. Coating over active leaks traps water — fix the path first.

DIY vs book

DIY

Seasonal walk-around, soft-sole shoes, photo soft spots, reseal minor lap sealant if you know the product.

Book UMRT

Soft decking, widespread seal failure, or mystery interior stains — Text with roof photos and leak location.

Owner checklist
  • Identify membrane vs metal vs fiberglass
  • Inspect every penetration after storms
  • Never walk a wet membrane in hard soles
  • Document soft spots before they grow

Field reality on the corridor

Measure before you buy parts

Forums love parts lists. A meter, a scale ticket, or a cold pressure check usually beats a guess. Bring photos when you Text Now — year, make, and a clear shot of the fault beat a long thread.

DIY boundary

Owners can inspect, document, and do basic setup from manufacturer manuals. Stop when you smell burning, see structural damage, or the fix needs sealed systems, gas work, or high-current DC you are not trained for.

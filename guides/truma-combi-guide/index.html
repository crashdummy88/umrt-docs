Truma Combi Furnace & Water Heater Troubleshooting Guide

Combines space heating and water heating in one unit — increasingly common in van builds, and not well documented outside Europe. Here’s what to check.

Active corridor: Montana · Wyoming · Idaho · Washington — case-by-case beyond.

Field notes

Combines space heating and water heating in one unit — increasingly common in van builds, and not well documented outside Europe. Here’s what to check. Check 12V supply voltage at the unit first — the Combi’s control board is sensitive to low voltage and will fault out well before the battery is actually “dead.” After that, check LP supply pressure and that the exhaust/intake vent isn’t obstructed — the unit’s airflow sensor will block ignition if it detects restricted venting.

Heats Water but Not Air, or Vice Versa

The Combi runs both functions off one burner but controls them independently through the digital control panel — check that both zones are actually switched on in the panel settings before assuming a hardware fault.

Error Codes on the Digital Panel

The Combi’s control panel displays a numeric fault code rather than a description.

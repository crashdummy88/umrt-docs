REFERENCE 25-00 · BATTERIES & ENERGY STORAGE Reference charging voltages for 12V nominal lead-acid (flooded/AGM) and LiFePO4 battery banks. Charging the wrong chemistry with the wrong profile is one of the most common causes of premature battery failure we see in the field.

Flooded & AGM Lead-Acid (12V Nominal)

Temperature compensation matters: lead-acid charging voltage should shift roughly −0.0167V per °F per cell (a 6-cell 12V battery) as ambient temperature rises or falls from 77°F (25°C).

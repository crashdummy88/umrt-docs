Field notes

Suburban and Atwood forced-air furnaces — the ducted heating system, not the water heater sharing the same brand name. This isolates the fault to ignition, not the blower motor. Check LP supply pressure, the sail switch (which confirms airflow before allowing ignition — a common failure point), and the igniter/electrode assembly for carbon buildup or a cracked ceramic.

Blower Runs but No Heat

This isolates the fault to ignition, not the blower motor.

Furnace Won’t Start at All

Check 12V supply first — furnace ignition boards are voltage-sensitive and won’t attempt ignition below a threshold the thermostat display may not warn you about.

Weak or Uneven Heat Through the Ducts

Usually a duct or register obstruction rather than a furnace fault — check that nothing is blocking floor registers, and that the return air path to the furnace itself is clear.

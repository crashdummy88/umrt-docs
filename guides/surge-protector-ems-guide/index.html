Field notes

The cheapest insurance policy in the entire RV, and one of the most skipped. Campground pedestal wiring varies wildly in age and quality. The two real threats are low/high voltage (an overloaded park circuit sagging under 108V, or occasionally spiking high) and open ground or reversed polarity, which is a shock hazard, not just an equipment risk. Neither trips a normal breaker — your rig’s AC, converter, and electronics just quietly take the damage.

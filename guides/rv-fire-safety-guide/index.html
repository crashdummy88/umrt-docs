Field notes

RV fires spread fast in a small, fuel- and propane-adjacent space — the margin between a contained incident and a total loss is often measured in seconds, which makes the right extinguisher, in the right place, genuinely life-or-property-critical.

Common ignition sources in an RV

Electrical faults — damaged wiring, overloaded circuits, failing converters — are among the most common causes, alongside propane leaks near an ignition source and cooking-related fires in the small galley.

Placement and inspection routine

Mount at least one extinguisher near the main exit — not the galley, where a kitchen fire could block access to it — and consider a second near the bedroom in larger rigs.

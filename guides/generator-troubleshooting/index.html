Generator · Troubleshooting

RV generator troubleshooting

Won’t start, won’t stay running, or runs with no power — those are three different jobs. Most of them start with stale fuel and a genset battery, not a new generator.

Generator maintenance starts at $150. Book on Square or ask in the shop.

A generator that sits for months grows varnish, gums jets, and kills its own start battery. Work the cheap list first. Do not keep cranking a flooded carb “to see.”

Won’t start

Fuel and shutoff

Level, valve position, and whether the coach is feeding the genset at all. Ethanol-blended gas gums a carb after roughly a month of sitting.

Genset battery

Not the house bank. A generator that clicks or cranks slow is often its own battery and cables.

Codes before clear

If the panel has a fault code, write it down before you reset anything.

Crank vs no-crank

Cranks but no fire: stale fuel, stuck choke, spark. No crank: breaker, door interlock, start battery, or a failed starter.

Starts, then dies — or runs with no power

Dies in seconds

Low-oil shutdown, fuel that fills the bowl then starves (filter/pump), gummed carb, or an ignition module that drops out.

Runs, no AC

Engine side is working. Look at the breaker, AVR, brushes, and whether the governor is hunting. This is not a “more starting fluid” problem.

Surges under the A/C

Governor or fuel delivery cannot hold load, or output voltage is already low before the compressor starts. Measure voltage and frequency under load, not at idle.

When to stop

Both stall-after-start and no-power get worse if you keep loading them. Book service rather than cooking the generator head.

Maintenance that prevents most of this

Exercise under load

At least two hours a month with a real load — not a 60 W bulb — if the genset is not in weekly use.

Fuel

Stabilizer if it sits more than 30 days, or run the carb dry. Keep the tank at least half full to cut condensation.

Oil and filter

OEM interval — commonly every 100–150 hours. Dusty corridor miles eat air filters faster than the book.

Service SKU

Published generator maintenance starts at $150. Labor is $150/hr when we are already on a quoted job.

Owner checklist
  • Check genset fuel valve and level
  • Load-test or at least voltage-check the genset start battery
  • Note fault codes before clearing
  • Do not keep spraying starter fluid into a flooded carb
  • Measure AC voltage and frequency under the load that fails

FAQ

House battery vs genset battery?

Different jobs. A healthy house bank will not crank a genset that has its own dead starting battery.

Can I use automotive gas?

If the OEM allows it. Ethanol still gums a sitting carb. Stabilize or use the fuel the manual names.

Onan code soup?

Write the code. Manufacturer charts live with the genset — we do not invent codes here.

DIY vs book?

Oil, filter, stabilizer, and a load run are owner work. No-power, governor, and fuel-system tear-down are the book line.

Remote support

How we connect

We use a few tools. The right one depends on the device.

Pick the device

There isn’t one app for every piece of gear in a rig. We match the tool to what we’re looking at.

  • United Mobile RV Connect for a Windows, Mac, or Raspberry Pi / Linux computer
  • Chrome Remote Desktop for a one-off paid session on a computer
  • TeamViewer when you already use it
  • Zoom or FaceTime for a phone, a tablet, or a camera walk-through
  • Peplink InControl2 for a Peplink router
  • Victron VRM for a Victron system on a Cerbo GX or other GX device
  • SSH for an OpenWrt or GL.iNet router, only with your OK

Packages are listed at unitedmobilerv.com/remote.

1. United Mobile RV Connect

This is our own remote-support app, built on the open-source MeshCentral project. We use it for Windows PCs and laptops, Macs, and Raspberry Pi or other Linux computers. It covers ongoing monitoring and one-time sessions.

We text or email you a private install link. It works for 7 days. You open it on the computer we’ll support. Each time we connect, you click Allow. Nothing happens until you do. A toolbar stays on screen while we’re connected, and you can disconnect anytime. After a one-time session we remove the app. Monitoring customers keep it installed.

Steps and pictures: Install United Mobile RV Connect.

2. Chrome Remote Desktop

For a one-off paid remote session on a computer, we often use Chrome Remote Desktop. You stay at the computer, generate a one-time code, and read it to us. The code only works once. You can stop sharing anytime.

Steps: Remote desktop setup, including the one-time code.

3. TeamViewer

If you already use TeamViewer, we can use that. You don’t have to switch. The short steps are on the remote desktop page.

4. Zoom or FaceTime screen share

Phones and tablets don’t run United Mobile RV Connect. iPhone, iPad, Chromebook, and Android phone help is a Zoom or FaceTime screen share.

The same call works when we need to see a panel, a breaker, or a screen in the rig. You point the camera, and we walk through it together.

5. Peplink InControl2

Peplink routers are cloud-managed. You grant us access to your device in InControl2. We don’t take the router off your account unless you ask us to. You can remove that access whenever you want.

6. Victron VRM

For a Victron system on a Cerbo GX or other GX device, you share VRM access with us. From there we can view the system and open the remote console. You stay the admin of your installation, and you can remove us in VRM whenever you want.

7. SSH for OpenWrt and GL.iNet

Some OpenWrt and GL.iNet routers are easier to check from a secure command line. We only do that with your permission, during a session you asked for. We don’t keep a way in after you tell us to stop.

Your OK comes first

We only connect when you ask, or when you approve it. Every session is logged. You can revoke access anytime: disconnect, uninstall, or tell us to remove our access and we’ll do it.

We don’t ask for your passwords by text or email. If a message like that shows up, don’t reply. Call us at (616) 606-5277.
